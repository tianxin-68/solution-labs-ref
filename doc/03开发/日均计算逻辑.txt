========================================================
日期不汇总：涉及调整内容包括：当前值计算、同环比值计算、自定义对比值计算
粒度=日，逻辑不变

粒度=周
- count(distinct) -> sum(count(distinct by dt))*1.0000/7
- sum() -> sum()*1.0000/7

粒度=月
- count(distinct) -> sum(count(distinct by dt))*1.0000/get_month_days(dt)
- sum() -> sum()*1.0000/get_month_days(dt)

粒度=年
- count(distinct) -> sum(count(distinct by dt))*1.0000/get_year_days(dt)
- sum() -> sum()*1.0000/get_year_days(dt)

日期汇总：涉及调整内容包括：当前值计算、自定义对比值计算
当前值
- count(distinct) -> sum(count(distinct by dt))/查询条件天数
- sum() -> sum()/查询条件天数

定义对比值
- count(distinct) -> sum(count(distinct by dt))/定义对比天数
- sum() -> sum()/定义对比天数
